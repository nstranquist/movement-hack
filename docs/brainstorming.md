# Brainstorming — Open Claw Hackathon

ETH Denver | February 19, 2025 | 5-hour window

---

## Simple & Polished (high demo quality)

- **On-chain polls/voting** — Create a poll, vote with your wallet, results stored on-chain
- **Tip jar / creator tipping** — Send MOVE tips to any address with a message on-chain
- **On-chain guestbook** — Sign with wallet, leave a message, all displayed in a feed

## More Interesting

- **Hackathon submission tracker** — Teams register on-chain, judges vote, leaderboard (meta + relevant)
- **On-chain raffle** — Buy tickets with MOVE, contract picks winner permissionlessly
- **Commit-reveal game** — Rock/paper/scissors or a simple guessing game between two wallets

## Agent-flavored (relevant to hackathon theme)

- **AI agent wallet** — A frontend where an AI agent can autonomously sign txs on your behalf
- **On-chain task bounty board** — Post tasks with MOVE bounty, claim when done
